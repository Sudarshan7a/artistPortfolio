
### **Homepage Layout**

1. **Framework and Tools**
   - The project uses **React with Vite** for a fast development environment.
   - Styling is handled using **Tailwind CSS** for utility-based classes, ensuring a clean and responsive layout.
   - Routing is managed with **React Router**, enabling seamless navigation between pages.

### **Structure and Components**
The homepage layout is broken down into the following components:

#### 1. **Header (Navigation Bar)**
- The header contains the **logo** on the left and **navigation links** on the right.
- Includes a **sticky layout** to remain visible at the top of the screen while scrolling.
- **Desktop View:** Inline navigation with space for up to five links.
- **Mobile View:** A collapsible menu (hamburger icon) for navigation.

#### 2. **Hero Section**
- Full-width section with a **background image or gradient**.
- Includes a **main heading (H1)**, a supporting **subheading**, and two **call-to-action (CTA) buttons** (e.g., View Gallery, Commission).
- The CTAs lead to specific sections or pages for further interaction.

#### 3. **Popular Featured Section**
- A **grid layout** showcasing featured artworks.
- Each grid item includes:
  - A **thumbnail image**.
  - A short **title** and description.
  - A link to learn more or view details.
- Responsive layout adjusts to one column on mobile devices.

#### 4. **About Section**
- Split layout:
  - **Left side:** Circular avatar or profile picture with a brief introduction.
  - **Right side:** Supporting text describing the artist’s expertise and style.
- Uses a centered layout with proper padding to maintain balance.

#### 5. **Scroll-Triggered Content Section**
- A dynamic, scroll-triggered section with **pinned images** on one side and **text content** on the other.
- As the user scrolls, the text changes to reveal new information while the images transition smoothly.

#### 6. **Gallery Section**
- A **carousel or grid layout** showcasing the portfolio of artwork.
- Each artwork is clickable, leading to a full-screen view or detail page.
- Incorporates hover effects for interactivity.

#### 7. **Footer**
- Includes:
  - Social media links and contact information.
  - A short CTA inviting visitors to connect or commission artwork.
  - Navigation links for easy access to other pages.
- Styled with a **clean and centered layout**, ensuring readability.

### **Responsive Design**
- Desktop:
  - Full-screen layout with clear separation of sections.
  - Uses margins and padding to maintain a balanced look.
- Tablet:
  - Grids and split layouts adjust to fewer columns.
  - Navigation switches to a smaller inline style or collapsible menu.
- Mobile:
  - All sections stack vertically for ease of scrolling.
  - Navigation switches to a hamburger menu for space efficiency.

### **Scroll Behavior and Animations**
- Smooth scrolling is implemented for internal links to ensure seamless navigation.
- The scroll-triggered content section uses animations for text transitions and image changes.
- A **sticky header** ensures navigation remains accessible throughout the page.

### **Additional Details**
- All images are **optimized for web use** to maintain performance.
- Uses **semantic HTML** for accessibility and search engine optimization (SEO).
- Tailwind’s **utility classes** simplify the process of adjusting spacing, layout, and responsiveness.
- Animations are lightweight and implemented using libraries like **Framer Motion** or **GSAP** if necessary.

### **Commission Page Structure**

1. **Header Section**
   - **Main Headline:** Positioned prominently at the top of the page, conveying the theme of commissioning unique artworks.
   - **Subtitle:** A short description that complements the headline, offering additional context.
   - **Commission Status Indicator:** Shows whether commissions are open or closed, with a bold visual emphasis.

2. **Custom Artwork Options Section**

     - Right: Services not offered.

   - A **grid layout** of cards representing different types of artwork available:
     - **Card Structure:**
       - Thumbnail or sample artwork.
       - Title of the artwork type (e.g., Full Body, Half-Body).
       - Short description or highlights.
       - Pricing details.
       - Status indicator (e.g., availability or conditions).

4. **Key Highlights Section**
   - Presented in a clean **info-box style** with a short summary of the advantages of choosing this service (e.g., no extra charge for basic backgrounds).

5. **Custom Artwork Showcase**
   - **Carousel Layout:**
     - Multiple horizontal sliders divided into artwork categories (e.g., "Characters," "Characters + Background").

       - Thumbnail image.

6. **Promotional Section**
   - Highlighting special offers such as:
     - Discounts or perks for orders exceeding a specific price point.
     - Specific promotional text emphasizing value (e.g., “Commissions over $800 include free alternate edits”).

7. **Footer Section**
   - A strong **Call to Action (CTA):** Encourages contacting the artist to begin a project.
   - Artist’s logo and name as a branding element.
   - Navigation links for quick access to other parts of the website.
   - Social media and communication links (e.g., Gmail, Twitter, Discord).

### **Page Layout Overview**

#### **1. Header Section**
- **Purpose**: Introduce the gallery with a welcoming tone.
- **Components**:
  - **Main Heading**: Positioned at the top with a clear call to explore.
  - **Subtitle**: Supporting text to elaborate on the gallery's purpose.
  - **Scroll Indicator**: A down-arrow animation to encourage interaction.
- **Styling**:
  - Center-aligned text with responsive padding.
  - Arrow uses Tailwind utilities for subtle `animate-bounce` behavior.

#### **2. Latest Artwork Highlight Section**
- **Purpose**: Display the most recent or notable artwork prominently.
- **Components**:
  - **Featured Artwork**: A large image with subtle styling (e.g., gradient border or shadow).
  - **Caption**: Two short lines of text:
    - Left: “Latest Artwork.”
    - Right: “If you can dream it, we can create it.”
- **Styling**:
  - Use Tailwind’s `grid` layout for alignment.
  - Add responsive scaling (`w-full`, `h-auto`) to ensure proper fit across devices.

#### **3. Masonry-Style Gallery Grid**
- **Purpose**: Showcase a wide variety of artwork in a dynamic layout.
- **Components**:
  - **Grid Layout**: Incorporates images of varying sizes for an engaging look.
  - **Hover Interaction**:
    - Images enlarge slightly on hover (`scale-105`).
    - Optional overlay with the artwork title or description fades in.
- **Styling**:
  - Use `grid-cols-2`, `grid-cols-3` for desktop, and `grid-cols-1` for mobile.
  - Tailwind’s `gap-x` and `gap-y` utilities for spacing.

#### **4. Featured Art Carousel**
- **Purpose**: Highlight select pieces interactively.
- **Components**:
  - **Horizontal Scrolling**: Users can swipe or click through artworks.
  - **Caption Overlay**: Brief description of the featured art.
  - **Snap Functionality**: Center alignment of images as the user scrolls.
- **Styling**:
  - Implement `overflow-x-scroll` for horizontal scrolling.
  - Use libraries like `react-slick` or Tailwind's `snap-mandatory` for snapping.

#### **5. Call-to-Action Section**
- **Purpose**: Encourage visitors to reach out or explore further.
- **Components**:
  - Large text prompt (e.g., "Your Art Awaits!").
  - Button linking to the **Contact Page**.
- **Styling**:
  - Use Tailwind’s `text-3xl` for prominence.
  - Buttons styled with `hover:bg-opacity-90` and smooth transitions.

### **Reusable Components for Implementation**

1. **Header Component**
   - Props: `title`, `subtitle`, optional scroll indicator toggle.
   - Use Tailwind’s `text-center` and spacing utilities.

2. **FeaturedArtwork Component**
   - Props: `image`, `caption`.
   - Handles hover effects using `transform` and `transition`.

3. **GalleryGrid Component**
   - Accepts a dynamic array of image objects with properties like `src`, `alt`, `title`.
   - Configurable for column counts based on screen sizes.

4. **Carousel Component**
   - Props: `artworkList`, `title`.
   - Scrolling behavior controlled via `overflow-x-scroll`.

5. **CTA Footer**
   - Reusable footer design with `text-center` alignment and navigation links.

### **Styling Details**
- **Responsive Design**: Tailwind’s breakpoints (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) ensure optimal layout adjustments.
- **Dark Backgrounds**: Contrast the artwork using a dark base (`bg-black`) with gradients for accents.
- **Hover Effects**: Subtle animations using `hover:scale`, `hover:shadow-lg`, and `transition-transform`.
